Traffic Control Mod 1.21.11-2.0.0 (Beta 1) - NeoForge 21.11.42 - Session Changelog (4/7/26) (1:50 PM)
======================================================================================================

### Commits
1. Started porting Traffic Control mod from Forge 1.12.2 to NeoForge 1.21.11
2. Added changes to changelog-1.21.11
3. Added some functionality to the horizontal pole (WIP)

### Traffic Light Frame Variants
- Added yellow traffic light frame variants (7 shapes: triple, single, double, quad, five, T, doghouse)
- Added orange traffic light frame variants (7 shapes: triple, single, double, quad, five, T, doghouse)
- Total: 21 frame items across black, yellow, and orange color families
- Frame item IDs changed from old mod format to new naming convention:
  - Black: traffic_light_frame, traffic_light_1_frame, traffic_light_2_frame, etc.
  - Yellow: yellow_traffic_light_frame, yellow_traffic_light_1_frame, etc.
  - Orange: orange_traffic_light_frame, orange_traffic_light_1_frame, etc.
- Frames now use ItemTrafficLightFrame class (extends BlockItem) instead of simple BlockItem

### Ghost Block Preview
- Added GhostBlockRenderer.java — renders a translucent (40% opacity) preview of the traffic light at the placement position whenever the player holds a traffic light frame item
- Shows at any position the player is looking at, not just near poles
- Dynamically shows/hides the horizontal bar based on whether poles are adjacent to the placement position
- Works for both main hand and offhand
- Applies correct rotation matching player facing direction
- Limited to 10 blocks max render distance
- Handles both block hits and air/miss hits (for creative flying)

### Extended Placement Range
- Modified ItemTrafficLightFrame.java — added BLOCK_INTERACTION_RANGE attribute modifier (+3.5 blocks) so traffic lights can be placed from 8 blocks away (survival) / 8.5 blocks (creative) instead of the default 4.5/5

### Horizontal Pole Hitbox
- Updated BlockHorizontalPole.java — selection shape (outline/hitbox) now matches the actual thin pole dimensions, with collision shape matching (reverted from full block)

### Horizontal Pole Model
- Extended horizontal_pole.json dimensions (Z=-9 to Z=25) so the bar properly connects to traffic light frames in all directions
- Signal arm bar in RotatableBlockEntityRenderer.java now uses horizontal_pole model for all directions

### Traffic Light Placement (WIP) (Beta 2)
- Traffic lights can be mounted on crossing gate poles, horizontal poles, and crossing gate bases
- Pole-mounted TLs shift 9/16 toward the pole for flush mounting
- Side-by-side TL rows connect flush (no pole shift when adjacent TLs detected)
- Back-to-back TL pairs across a pole block are detected and handled
- TLs become transparent (click-through) when holding a TL frame for easy row placement
- Hitbox uses split top/bottom shapes for solo pole-mounted TLs, normal shapes for side-by-side rows
- HP placement logic in ItemTrafficLightFrame finds empty positions around horizontal poles
- CG_POLE connects to BlockHorizontalPole, BlockTrafficLight, BlockCrossingGatePole via blockstate arms
- HP connects to BlockTrafficLight, BlockCrossingGatePole, BlockCrossingGateBase, BlockHorizontalPole
- HP renders ext arms toward adjacent TLs, CG poles, and CG bases
- Non-cardinal TL rotations use short pole arm model (traffic_light_pole_arm) to prevent bar poke-through
- TL-to-pole connecting bar skipped for non-cardinal rotations to avoid clipping through angled body
- Added new models: signal_arm_bar.json, traffic_light_paired.json, traffic_light_pole_arm.json
- Removed redundant full-length bar from TL toward horizontal pole (HP already renders short arm, TL shift bridges the gap)
- Added short pole arm from TL toward crossing gate pole for CG-pole-mounted TLs
- Fixed gap when TL is between HP and CG pole: TL no longer shifts when it has pole connections on both sides
- HP now renders ext arms toward back-to-back TL pairs for proper pole connection (same as crossing_gate_pole)
- Fixed bug: TL hitboxes now always visible/breakable even when holding a TL frame item
- Removed signal_arm block, item, model, and blockstate (replaced by direct connections between traffic lights, crossing gate poles/bases, and horizontal poles)

### Traffic Light Bulbs
- Added all 18 traffic light bulb items as separate registered items (NeoForge 1.21 no longer supports metadata-based item variants)
- Basic bulbs: Red, Yellow, Green
- Left arrow bulbs: Red, Yellow, Green Arrow Left
- Right arrow bulbs: Red, Yellow, Green Arrow Right
- Straight arrow bulbs: Straight Red, Straight Yellow, Straight Green
- Pedestrian bulbs: Cross, Don't Cross
- No turn bulbs: No Right Turn, No Left Turn
- Tunnel bulbs: Tunnel Green, Tunnel Red (restored from v0.4.1)
- Fixed texture paths from blocks/ to block/ for NeoForge 1.21 compatibility
- Added item definition files in assets/trafficcontrol/items/ (required by NeoForge 1.21)
- All bulbs appear in the Traffic Control creative tab with 3D models and textures

### New Creative Tab Items
- Added 19 placeholder blocks to the creative tab: Pedestrian Button, Crossing Gate, Crossing Lamps, Crossing Gate Crossbuck, Overhead (Pole), Overhead, Overhead Lamps, Overhead Crossbuck, Sign, Upper Quadrant Wig Wag, Lower Quadrant Wig Wag, SafeTran Type 3 Electronic Bell, SafeTran Mechanical Bell, WCH Mechanical Bell, WCH Electronic Bell, Street Sign, Traffic Sensor (Left), Traffic Sensor (Straight), Traffic Sensor (Right)
- Fixed all old Forge blockstates to NeoForge 1.21 format
- Fixed texture paths from blocks/ to block/ for all new block models
- Added sensor display transforms matching old mod's inventory rendering
- Street sign uses flat 2D item model with item/generated parent

### Sign Block
- BlockSign now uses ROTATION_16 for 16-direction rotation (was HorizontalDirectionalBlock with 4 directions)
- Sign faces toward the player when placed, supports all 16 angles like traffic lights
- RenderShape.INVISIBLE — block entity renderer handles body + arms with cutout render type
- Sign inventory icon uses flat 2D circle texture instead of 3D block model
- Separated block model (sign_block.json, pole only) from item model (sign_item.json, pole + static face)

### Sign GUI
- Ported sign selection GUI from 1.12.2 GuiScreen to NeoForge 1.21 Screen API
- Right-click sign block to open GUI with searchable grid of all 900+ sign textures
- Search box filters signs by name in real-time using EditBox with setResponder
- Sign grid renders 32×32 thumbnails with 4-column layout and scroll support
- Mouse wheel scrolling and draggable scrollbar for navigating the full sign list
- Blue hover highlight and green selection highlight (2px borders) on sign thumbnails
- Hovered sign name displayed at bottom of grid panel
- Left panel shows large preview of selected sign with name, type, and note text
- Default blank circle sign rendered when no sign is selected
- Sign selection saved to SignBlockEntity and synced to server via PacketUpdateSign on GUI close

### Sign Renderer (Dynamic)
- Sign face texture rendered dynamically by RotatableBlockEntityRenderer using submitCustomGeometry
- Front face rendered with entityCutout render type at z=0.431, facing -z (north in local space)
- Back face rendered 0.01 behind front face at z=0.441, facing +z (south in local space)
- Both faces rotate with the block's ROTATION_16 property
- Sign textures loaded on-demand via ensureTextureLoaded (registers SimpleTexture if not already loaded)
- signFrontTexture and signBackTexture fields added to RotatableBlockEntityRenderState
- Falls back to DEFAULT_BLANK_SIGN texture when no sign is selected on the block entity

### Sign Pole Connections & Mounting (WIP) (Beta 2)
- Signs render short arms (traffic_light_pole_arm model) toward adjacent CG poles, CG bases, and traffic lights
- Signs do NOT render arms toward horizontal poles — the HP renders its own short arm toward the sign
- HP renders short arm (traffic_light_pole_arm) toward adjacent signs to avoid double-arm overlap
- Signs shift 9/16 toward CG pole/base when mounted (not toward HP — stays centered)
- Sign face, back face, and arms all shift with body when mounted on CG pole
- Sign-to-sign chaining: signs detect adjacent signs and render short arms (horizontal_bar_connect, 2px)
- Chained signs detect CG pole 2 blocks away (through adjacent sign) and shift toward it
- Back-to-back signs: adjacent signs facing opposite directions (rotation diff of 8) snap together
- Both shift 9/16 toward each other, no arms rendered between them
- Signal arm bar bridge rendered between the two poles
- Rotation-aware hitbox: thin slab for cardinal rotations, full block for diagonal rotations
- Hitbox shifts with body when mounted on CG pole/base or chained
- No collision shape — players walk through signs

### Traffic Light Back-to-Back
- Adjacent traffic lights facing opposite directions (rotation diff of 8) now snap together
- Both shift 9/16 toward each other with signal arm bar bridge between poles
- Horizontal bar skipped between back-to-back TLs (bridge renders instead)

### Screwdriver Item
- Ported screwdriver from 1.12.2 to NeoForge 1.21.11
- Right-click on any Traffic Control block to rotate clockwise (ROTATION_16 +1 or FACING 90°)
- Shift+right-click to rotate counter-clockwise
- 128 durability, plays screwdriver sound on use, no damage in creative
- 3D item model with existing textures (screwdriver_top, screwdriver_metal, shunt)
- Tooltip: "Hold SHIFT for more info" expands to full description
- Sign GUI suppressed when holding screwdriver (block checks for screwdriver in hand)
